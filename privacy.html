Golf Culture — Privacy Policy

Effective 11 Sep 2026 Version 1.0 Applies to the Golf Culture TikTok account & posting tool
Plain English, before the clauses Golf Culture reposts golf video clips to TikTok with the original creator's permission. The only account data we touch is what TikTok requires to publish a video to our own account. We don't collect data from people who watch our videos, and we don't sell anything to anyone.
01

What this is

This policy covers Golf Culture, a TikTok account operated by one person, and the small local software tool used to run it. The tool downloads golf video content that the original creator has given explicit permission to repost, edits it into short-form clips, and publishes those clips to the Golf Culture TikTok account.

02

Information we access

To publish videos, the tool uses TikTok's Content Posting API under an authorization (OAuth) token connected to the Golf Culture account only. That access is used strictly to:

  • Upload a video file to the Golf Culture account
  • Set that video's caption/description
  • Confirm a post published successfully

The tool does not read, collect, or store data belonging to other TikTok accounts, viewers, or followers.

03

Where our source video comes from

Clips originate from other creators' videos (for example, on YouTube), used only with that creator's explicit, on-record permission to repost. Golf Culture does not source or republish content without that permission.

04

Information we do not collect

We do not:

  • Track or profile people who view Golf Culture's TikTok posts
  • Collect analytics beyond what TikTok's own creator tools report to us
  • Sell, rent, or share any data with third parties
  • Use TikTok data for advertising or targeting purposes
05

How we store data

API credentials (the authorization tokens described in section 02) are stored locally on the operator's own machine, not on a shared server, and are not transmitted anywhere except directly to TikTok's API to publish a post. Downloaded source video and rendered clips are kept locally for editing and review before posting.

06

Third-party services

Golf Culture's tool interacts with YouTube (to retrieve permitted source video) and TikTok (to publish clips). Each of those platforms handles data under its own privacy policy; this document only covers what Golf Culture itself does.

07

Changes to this policy

If how Golf Culture handles data changes, this page will be updated and the version number and effective date above will change accordingly.

08

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to golfculturetiktok@gmail.com.
